## Rules

- **One studio.** Every screen carries `SampleTag` ("Sample studio"). No studio name, city, logo or invented brand.
- **One day.** Thursday, Oct 8, 2026, 10:40. Use `TODAY_LABEL` / `TODAY_DATE_LINE` / `NOW`. Any new date: check the weekday first (valid: Mon Oct 5, Tue Oct 6, Wed Oct 7, Thu Oct 8, Fri Oct 9, Sat Oct 10, Thu Nov 5, Sat Nov 7, Tue Nov 10, Fri Nov 13, Sat Nov 14; every date already in `sample-data.ts` was checked). Anything that has already happened must be before Thu 10:40.
- **Numbers come from `sample-data.ts`.** Totals are computed there (`DEPOSITS_HELD_TODAY_CENTS` = $340 = Asha's $240 pool + Jo's $100, `DEPOSITS_APPLIED_TODAY_CENTS` = $80 (Priya), `WEEK_GROSS_CENTS` = $7,630, `WEEK_PAID_OUT_CENTS` = $5,622, `IN_DEPOSIT_POOLS_CENTS` = $600, `COMMISSIONS_OWED_CENTS` = $1,236 …). Never type a total by hand; format with `usd()`.
- **Three money windows, one set of books.** `DAILY_REVENUE` (Sep 9 – today) feeds all three: the payout week Sep 28 – Oct 4 (`WEEK_GROSS_CENTS` $7,630), the last 30 days (`REVENUE_30D_CENTS` $27,190, Analytics' default range) and this calendar month, Oct 1 – today (`REVENUE_MONTH_CENTS` $7,780 and `BOOKINGS_MONTH` 49, the Locations rollup). Say which window a figure is for.
- **Policies.** No cancellation window is enforced in the app (it is saved, not checked), so never write that a late cancel keeps a deposit automatically: the studio decides whether a deposit is kept or refunded (Leo B.'s was kept by the studio). Deposit requests lapse after `DEPOSIT_DEADLINE_HOURS` (24, the app's `studios.deposit_deadline_hours` default).
- **The app's words.** Labels, tabs, statuses and columns below are copied from the app source listed. Say "Projects" (not "Sleeves"), "session", "consult", "deposit pool", "red ink". Messages channels: SMS and email only.
- **Nothing the app doesn't have:** no no-show risk score, healing/sterilization blocks, loyalty programme, "Studio P&L", portfolio auto-sync, Apprentice role.
- **AI never draws or drafts.** `AiMoodboardScreen` is the AI screen's composer as the app has it today: Client, "The brief" (the artist's own words), Style chips, Placement & size, Save draft, and a disabled "AI generation not available" button; the saved design's moodboard is empty ("No references on this moodboard. Generation isn't connected yet…") and has no Present button. Client references live on the project's Moodboard tab. Never label anything "Drafted" or AI-written.
- **Never fake tattoo art.** Photos are `PhotoTile` (canvas-deep block + label "S1", "P2", "F3": serif letter, Inter number, because the serif 1 reads as l). Small slots use `PhotoLabel size="sm"`. A named design passes `title` (and `subtitle`, its style): the tile prints "Dagger and rose / Traditional" large at the bottom-left instead of the code, so a flash sheet reads as a priced list; `aspect="wide"` is a short 3:2 plate.
- **One set of 30-day figures.** The last 30 days (Wed, Sep 9 – Thu, Oct 8) live in `sample-data.ts` (`SESSIONS_30D` 60, `BOOKINGS_30D` 69, `BOOKINGS_BY_DAY_30D` (sums to 69), `NEW_CLIENTS_30D` 23 …). Analytics, Forms (signed in 30 days, never more than the bookings) and Marketing segments all read them.
- **Walk-ins are bookings.** A walk-in who sits gets a booking in the app (then its payment, then a commission record). Rio's four on Tue, Oct 6 are four "Walk-in" rows on Appointments (This week, Completed), one "4 walk-ins" card counted as 4 on the calendar week (Tue: 5 bookings), four ledger rows on Payments, and part of Rio's 13 bookings on Guest artists and Today's guest banner. Open walk-in slots (today's, Friday's) aren't bookings yet.
- **Decorative.** `AppFrame` and phone shells are `aria-hidden`. Inside a mockup use only `span`/`div`/`p`/table markup: no headings, links, buttons or inputs.
- **Mobile.** Phone screens stay phone-sized (`AppShellPhone`). Dense tables go in `AppTable` (scrolls inside the frame, sticky header, right-edge fade; pick `minWidth`). Card layouts stack with container queries on the frame body (`@md:`, `@xl:`, `@2xl:` …), never viewport breakpoints. The page itself must never scroll sideways.
- **Colour.** App chrome uses the `app-*` tokens only (sand sidebar, peach active pill, status tones). The one extra colour is the app's rust primary, which lives inside `AppButton variant="primary"`. Public app pages (booking, consent, portal) use `variant="onyx"`.
